# Research: How Remote Work Is Narrowing Early-Career Opportunities

> **remote-jobhunt-guide · Media Report**
> **Source**: Harvard Business Review
> **Original**: https://hbr.org/2026/08/research-how-remote-work-is-narrowing-early-career-opportunities
> **Published**: 2026-08
> **Collected**: 2026-08-08
> **Core finding**: Remote job postings require ~25% more skills, more years of experience, and higher credentials than otherwise identical in-person roles (50M+ postings + 37 interviews). Root cause: remote work weakens on-the-job training and informal learning, so employers raise hiring bars to compensate. Key conclusion: remote work, not AI, is the primary driver of declining early-career opportunities.

---

Imagine that somewhere, a hiring manager faces a decision between two candidates for a remote position. One is a recent graduate: competent, motivated, and full of potential. The other has accumulated five years of experience and can hit the ground running. Not long ago, managers would regularly take a chance on the graduate, confident that the organization could turn raw potential into exceptional performance. The right team, strong mentors, and moments of informal learning can transform a promising newcomer into a high-performing professional.

想象一下，在某个地方，一位招聘经理正面临着为一个远程岗位从两名候选人中做出选择的决策。其中一位是刚毕业的学生：能力不错，有上进心，且潜力十足。另一位则积累了五年的工作经验，能迅速上手开展工作。不久前，经理们通常会给这位毕业生一个机会，他们相信公司能够将其潜力转化为出色的工作表现。合适的团队、优秀的导师，以及一些非正式的学习契机，都能将一位有潜力的新人转变为一名高效能的专业人士。

In our recent research, published in Administrative Science Quarterly, we found that, for remote positions, companies have become far less willing to take a chance on rookie candidates.

We analyzed more than 50 million job postings across 28 European countries and conducted 37 in-depth interviews with hiring managers and executives. We found that remote positions consistently required about 25% more skills, more years of work experience, and higher educational credentials than otherwise identical non-remote positions from the same employer, with the same job title, in the same year. The only difference is where the work is done. We replicated this pattern in a controlled experiment with 1,200 hiring managers recruited online, who set requirements for the same job randomly described as either remote or in-person. The pattern held: simply labeling a job remote pushed companies to demand more skills and more experience.

我们分析了28个欧洲国家超过5000万条招聘信息，并对招聘经理和高管进行了37次深度访谈。我们发现，与同一家雇主、同年发布的、职位名称相同的非远程岗位相比，远程岗位始终要求多出约25%的技能、更多年的工作经验以及更高的学历。唯一的区别在于工作地点。我们在一项对照实验中重现了这一模式。该实验招募了1200名线上招聘经理，他们为同一份工作设定要求，这份工作被随机描述为远程或现场办公。这种模式依然存在：仅仅将一份工作标记为远程，就会促使公司要求更多技能和经验。

Our study found that the shift in requirements is primarily driven by three reinforcing forces: larger applicant pools, an increased reliance on measurable credentials, and the training challenges inherent in remote work.

The first two are self-explanatory. Remote work passively expands applicant pools, with companies often receiving anywhere from 20% to ten times more applicants than comparable in-person roles. This has prompted employers to raise hiring thresholds simply to narrow the field. Second, when face-to-face interaction offers fewer opportunities to assess potential and cultural fit directly, some employers may place greater weight on verifiable credentials, such as degrees, certifications, and documented experience. Though the evidence for this mechanism is mixed, our interviews suggest that it shapes hiring decisions in ways beyond what job postings alone may capture.

前两个原因不言而喻。远程工作无形中扩大了求职者群体，与同等的线下岗位相比，企业收到的简历数量往往多出 20% 到 10 倍不等。这促使雇主提高招聘门槛，以缩小筛选范围。其次，当面对面交流无法直接评估求职者的潜力和与企业文化的契合度时，一些雇主可能会更看重学历、证书和工作经历等可验证的资质。虽然这一机制的证据并不统一，但我们的访谈表明，它在招聘决策中的影响超出了招聘启事本身所体现的范围。

However, we believe that by far the most consequential factor at play here, and the one that leaders urgently need to recognize, is the way remote work has hindered on-the-job training, making hiring managers seek out those who already have experience. We argue that the problem is not with remote work per se, but with the short-sighted decision-making companies are falling back on when it comes to hiring for remote positions. In this article, we’ll explain why this bias occurs and what leaders can do to build more sustainable talent pipelines.

然而，我们认为，目前在这方面影响最大、且领导者亟需认识到的因素是，远程工作阻碍了在职培训，使得招聘经理倾向于招聘有经验的人。我们认为，问题并不在于远程工作本身，而在于公司在招聘远程岗位时采取了短视的决策方式。在本文中，我们将解释这种偏见产生的原因，以及领导者可以采取哪些措施来构建更具可持续性的人才储备渠道。

High-potential, inexperienced talent has not become less valuable, but hiring leaders in our interview study explained firsthand how organizational conditions that once nurtured it have quietly disappeared in remote work environments. They’re referring to the hallway conversations that resolve questions before they became problems, the shoulder-tap corrections after missteps, the tacit knowledge gained simply by working alongside more experienced colleagues. These informal, spontaneous learning opportunities, leaders said, are way harder to replicate remotely. As one manager in our study put it: “It would have been smarter to recruit someone for that position who had established experience instead of putting someone new in that role.”

高潜力的新手人才并非贬值了，但在我们的访谈研究中，招聘负责人亲述，曾经培育这类人才的组织环境，在远程办公模式下已悄然消失。他们指的是那些在问题还未形成时就将其解决的走廊闲谈、犯错后及时给予的提醒纠正，以及仅仅通过与经验更丰富的同事共事就能获得的隐性知识。领导们表示，这些非正式的、自发的学习机会，在远程办公时极难重现。我们研究中的一位经理这样说道：“那个岗位招聘有经验的人会更明智，而不是安排一个新手。”

As developing new hires becomes more challenging, organizations are more likely to hire experienced workers who require less development in the first place. This fear is justified, as recent studies point to the same shift. Economists studying software engineers at a Fortune 500 firm from 2019 to 2024 found that simply sitting near teammates increased the opportunities of informal mentoring and improved performance, with the largest benefits accruing to younger, less-experienced employees. Once the offices closed, the study found, such opportunities largely disappeared. Extending this finding, researchers who analyzed national U.S. labor market data found that young college graduates experienced a sharper unemployment rise after the pandemic—yet only in occupations that can be performed remotely.

随着培养新员工变得愈发困难，企业更倾向于招聘那些一开始就无需太多培养的有经验的员工。这种担忧不无道理，因为近期的研究也指出了同样的趋势。研究人员对一家《财富》500 强公司 2019 年至 2024 年间软件工程师的情况进行了分析，发现仅仅是与同事相邻而坐，就能增加获得非正式指导的机会，提升工作表现，其中年轻且经验不足的员工受益最大。研究发现，一旦办公室关闭，这类学习机会就基本上消失了。研究人员进一步分析了美国全国劳动力市场数据后发现，疫情过后，年轻大学毕业生的失业率大幅上升，但这一现象仅出现在可以远程完成的工作岗位上。

These increased training difficulties in remote environments are reflected in the responses of managers in our own study. “Because in a remote setting,” one HR manager explained, “it’s a lot harder to find an organic mentor within the organization who can help develop the skill sets of team members. So, when folks are working remotely, we need people that know what they’re doing.” Another respondent put it more starkly: “Being fully remote, especially as an entry-level employee, is really hard … you learn in person in your first few jobs how to interact and find mentorship and do all of those intangibles and get to know folks. I can’t imagine having just been dropped into a remote world and said, ‘Good luck’…”

远程办公环境下培训难度的增加，在我们研究中经理们的反馈里得到了体现。一位人力资源经理解释道：“在远程办公的环境下，很难在公司内部找到合适的导师来帮助团队成员提升技能。”所以，员工远程办公时，我们需要那些能独当一面的人。另一位受访者说得更直白：“完全远程办公，尤其是对于初级员工来说，真的很难……刚开始工作的那几年，你需要通过面对面交流来学习如何与人交往、寻找导师，掌握这些难以言喻的能力，并且结识同事。”我无法想象直接把一个人丢进远程办公的环境，然后对他说‘祝你好运’……”

In addition to training challenges, a Science study which followed 588,000 American workers over more than a decade, also revealed that physical isolation arising from remote work—even years after the Covid-19 pandemic—is associated with measurable negative consequences for mental health, particularly for those early-career workers, the same workers already losing ground in a hiring market that increasingly favors experience over potential. Our own interviews surfaced the same dynamic from the inside. “Without the informal interactions and opportunities for social bonding,” one respondent told us, “It’s just hard to build strong connections with the manager and other senior people.” Another put it more bluntly: “It is much harder to make someone feel like they are truly part of the team and culture when everyone is remote.”

除了培训挑战之外，《科学》杂志的一项研究对58.8万名美国员工进行了长达十多年的跟踪调查。结果显示，远程工作导致的物理隔离——即使在新冠疫情结束多年后——也会对心理健康造成明显的负面影响，尤其是对那些处于职业生涯早期的员工而言。而在如今的招聘市场上，经验越来越受到青睐，潜力则被置于次要位置，这些员工本就已经处于劣势。我们自己的访谈也从内部揭示了同样的情况。一位受访者告诉我们：“没有了非正式的交流和社交互动的机会，就很难与经理和其他资深员工建立起牢固的关系。”另一位受访者则更直白地说：“当所有人都远程办公时，很难让员工真正融入团队和企业文化。”

Together, these studies share a common thread: while remote work offers clear benefits, it may also carry severe, less visible consequences by subtly reshaping how early-career professionals learn, grow, connect, and ultimately transform into high-performing employees. These are not separate challenges but parts of the same reinforcing cycle. As organizations find it harder to develop inexperienced employees remotely, they increasingly hire people who require less development. That, in turn, leaves fewer opportunities for the next generation to gain the very experience employers now demand, while weaker mentoring, slower learning, and greater isolation further erode development and wellbeing.

这些研究都有一个共同点：虽然远程工作有明显的好处，但它也可能带来严重且不易察觉的后果，因为它会潜移默化地改变职场新人学习、成长、建立人际关系的方式，最终影响他们成长为高绩效员工的进程。这些问题并非孤立存在，而是同一恶性循环的不同环节。由于企业发现远程培养缺乏经验的员工难度较大，因此他们越来越倾向于招聘那些无需太多培养的人。这反过来又导致下一代获得雇主所需经验的机会减少，同时，指导不足、学习缓慢和孤立感加剧等问题，会进一步阻碍他们的职业发展，影响他们的身心健康。

A few caveats are worth noting. Our job posting evidence comes from European labor markets, although our experiment and interviews with U.S. professionals point to the same underlying pattern. The effects we documented were also most pronounced in fully remote arrangements: in our data, hybrid roles resembled in-person positions far more than fully remote ones, suggesting that even periodic in-person interactions may preserve much of the developmental infrastructure at stake. The evidence on social connection and mental health comes from complementary studies, not our own research. While those studies consistently document strong associations, they do not establish that remote work causes these outcomes. These nuances matter because they suggest that the primary risks, and the key opportunities for intervention, lie in fully remote teams, particularly those with early-career employees.

有几点需要注意的事项值得一提。我们关于招聘信息的证据来自欧洲劳动力市场，不过我们在美国专业人士中开展的实验和访谈也指向了相同的潜在模式。我们所记录的影响在完全远程办公的安排中也最为明显：在我们的数据里，混合办公模式的岗位与现场办公岗位的相似程度远高于完全远程办公岗位，这表明即使是偶尔的面对面交流，也可能在很大程度上维持关键的职业发展环境。关于社交联系和心理健康的证据来自其他补充性研究，而非我们自己的研究。虽然这些研究一致记录了两者之间的紧密关联，但它们并未证实远程工作会导致这些结果。这些细微差别很重要，因为它们表明主要风险以及关键的干预机会存在于完全远程办公的团队中，尤其是那些有职场新人的团队。

It is tempting to attribute the recent decline in early-career hiring to the rise of generative AI. The evidence, however, suggests that remote work is a more robust explanation. A recent study examining both AI exposure and remote work simultaneously found that while both independently predicted declines in junior hiring, the remote work effect remained robust when the two were estimated together, whereas the AI effect largely disappeared. Remote work, not AI, appeared to be the primary driver of declining opportunities for early-career workers, at least for now. This does not rule out future effects of AI, but it suggests that changes in how organizations work, not just the technologies they adopt, are already reshaping the talent pipeline.

人们很容易将近期早期职业岗位招聘数量的下降归咎于生成式 AI 的兴起。然而，有证据表明，远程工作才是更合理的解释。近期一项同时研究 AI 应用和远程工作的研究发现，虽然二者各自都能预示初级岗位招聘数量的下降，但当同时考量这两个因素时，远程工作的影响依然显著，而 AI 的影响则基本消失了。至少就目前而言，远程工作而非 AI，似乎才是导致早期职业工作者机会减少的主要原因。这并不排除 AI 未来会带来影响，但它表明，组织工作方式的改变，而非仅仅是所采用的技术，已经在重塑人才输送渠道。

Rebuilding the social and developmental infrastructure that physical proximity once provided will not be accomplished by simply raising hiring standards or tweaking individual policies. It is, rather, one of the key challenges for remote work firms. The question is: how can managers do it?

Recalibrate hiring standards deliberately as credential creep is not a sustainable strategy.

Our research suggests that employers raise skill and experience requirements for remote roles largely as a response to weaker training capacity and larger applicant pools. In many cases, these higher standards are less about what the job truly requires than about compensating for organizational capabilities that have become harder to provide remotely. Managers should therefore scrutinize every requirement in a remote job description. Simply ask: Is this capability essential on day one, or is it compensating for mentoring and development we are no longer confident we can deliver? Requirements tied to genuinely independent, high-stakes work should remain. Those added primarily to reduce applicant volume or substitute credentials for managerial judgment deserve closer examination.

我们的研究表明，雇主提高远程岗位的技能和经验要求，很大程度上是为了应对培训能力的不足和大量的求职者。在很多情况下，这些更高的标准并非真正取决于工作本身的要求，而是为了弥补那些难以通过远程方式提供的组织能力。因此，管理者应该仔细审查远程岗位描述中的每一项要求。只需问问自己：这项能力是否在入职第一天就必不可少，还是说它是为了弥补我们不再有信心提供的指导和培养？与真正需要独立完成且风险较高的工作相关的要求应该保留。那些主要为了减少求职者数量，或者用证书替代管理判断而增加的要求，值得进一步审视。

The goal is not to lower standards indiscriminately, but to distinguish between requirements driven by the work itself and those driven by the organization’s diminished capacity to develop talent. Organizations that rebuild effective mentoring and onboarding can afford to hire more for potential and reduce their dependence on an increasingly scarce and expensive market for fully formed talent.

我们的目标并非不加区分地降低标准，而是要区分哪些要求是工作本身所决定的，哪些是因组织培养人才的能力下降而提出的。那些重建有效指导和新员工入职培训体系的组织，能够更多地依据潜力来招聘人才，减少对成熟人才市场的依赖，毕竟这类人才日益稀缺且成本高昂。

Our research suggests that the rise in hiring requirements reflects a structural shift of how work is organized. This requires systematically rebuilding the developmental infrastructure that proximity once provided. Formalizing mentoring could be a good starting point. Pair every new remote hire with an experienced senior colleague whose role extends beyond answering questions to sharing tacit knowledge, judgment, context, and informal norms that employees once absorbed simply by working alongside others. Make these conversations a core part of onboarding rather than an optional extra.

我们的研究表明，招聘要求的提高反映了工作组织方式的结构性转变。这需要系统地重建曾经因近距离办公而具备的发展支撑体系。将导师制度规范化或许是个不错的开端。为每一位新入职的远程员工配对一位经验丰富的资深同事，这位同事的职责不仅仅是回答问题，还包括分享隐性知识、判断力、工作背景信息以及非正式规范，这些内容员工曾经只需与他人并肩工作就能自然习得。要将这些交流对话作为入职培训的核心内容，而非可选项。

More importantly, consider rotating mentors periodically to expose new hires to a broader range of expertise and perspectives. This also helps distribute the mentoring workload across the organization. Doing so also recreates the broader learning environment that once emerged naturally when people worked in close proximity.

Remote work can increase psychological distress, but its effects rarely appear all at once. Instead, they accumulate gradually through the slow erosion of everyday social interactions. Organizations may create structured opportunities for in-person connection, preserve time for informal, virtual interactions, and develop team rituals that foster belonging.

远程工作会加重心理负担，但这种影响很少会立刻显现出来。相反，它是通过日常社交互动的逐渐减少而慢慢累积的。企业可以创造有组织的面对面交流机会，预留出进行非正式线上互动的时间，并制定能培养归属感的团队惯例。

First, periodic in-person gatherings, such as quarterly off-sites, onboarding retreats, or cross-functional learning days, serve a purpose that video calls cannot replicate. Research shows that periodic face-to-face interaction strengthens trust, facilitates richer knowledge exchange, and builds more enduring bonds that continue to benefit collaboration after employees return to distributed work. A learning retreat, for example, where junior and senior employees work through real challenges side by side, can achieve in days what months of virtual meetings rarely accomplish. Second, deliberately create opportunities for informal interaction. Remote work dramatically reduces the spontaneous conversations through which employees build relationships, exchange knowledge, and solve problems. Regular virtual coffee chats, rotating peer conversations, open office hours, and a few agenda-free minutes before or after meetings can help restore these low-stakes interactions. The objective is not to recreate the office, but to recreate the informal connections that remote work makes less likely. Third, teams can also establish simple but consistent rituals that create ambient belonging. Recurring team rituals such as weekly reflections, rotating project walkthroughs, or regularly celebrating small wins, can strengthen cohesion and reinforce employees’ sense of belonging. The key is consistency and genuine participation as rituals derive their value from being meaningful collective practices rather than performative exercises.

首先，定期的面对面聚会，如季度异地会议、入职培训静修会或跨部门学习日，有着视频通话无法复制的作用。研究表明，定期的面对面交流能够增强信任，促进更丰富的知识交流，建立更持久的关系，这些在员工回归远程工作后仍将对协作有所助益。例如，在一次学习静修活动中，新老员工并肩应对实际挑战，几天内取得的成果可能是数月的线上会议都难以达成的。其次，要有意识地创造非正式交流的机会。远程工作极大地减少了员工之间的即兴交流，而这些交流有助于建立关系、交换知识和解决问题。定期的线上咖啡闲聊、轮流进行的同伴交流、开放办公时间，以及会议前后留出的无议程几分钟，都有助于恢复这种轻松的交流。目的并非重现办公室场景，而是重建因远程工作而难以形成的非正式联系。第三，团队还可以建立简单但持续的仪式，营造一种归属感。像每周反思会、轮流进行的项目汇报，或定期庆祝小成就等重复性的团队仪式，能够增强凝聚力，强化员工的归属感。关键在于持续性和真诚参与，因为仪式的价值源于有意义的集体实践，而非形式化的活动。

Remote work changes not only where and how people work, but also what managers can observe. When day-to-day work is less visible, it is easy to rely on imprecise signals. Those who respond fast, who are always online, who speak most often in virtual meetings, or who simply appear busiest tend to get more recognition, largely due to visibility effects. Yet, these cues could be inaccurate measures of work contributions and can disadvantage employees whose work is less visible yet equally valuable. Managers need to make performance goals and expectations explicit, define performance standards based on tangible outcomes rather than activity, evaluate projects against documented contributions and measurable results, and ensure that promotion decisions reward output and impact rather than constant availability. Regular feedback and transparent evaluation criteria can further reduce the temptation to substitute visibility for performance.

远程工作不仅改变了人们的工作地点和方式，还改变了管理者的可观察范围。当日常工作的可见度降低时，人们很容易依赖不准确的信号。那些响应迅速、始终在线、在虚拟会议中发言最频繁，或者只是看起来最忙碌的人往往更容易获得认可，这在很大程度上是由于可见性效应。然而，这些信号可能无法准确衡量员工的工作贡献，并且可能使那些工作不太显眼但同样有价值的员工处于劣势。管理者需要明确绩效目标和期望，根据实际成果而非工作表现来制定绩效标准，对照记录在案的贡献和可衡量的结果评估项目，并确保晋升决策奖励的是工作产出和影响力，而非随时待命的状态。定期反馈和透明的评估标准可以进一步减少用可见度替代绩效的诱惑。

The hidden costs of remote work are not inevitable. They arise because many organizations have changed where people work without rethinking how people learn, develop, and build meaningful connections. The hiring decision of choosing an experienced candidate over a promising talent will continue to happen. But managers may choose differently and optimally when they understand the forces behind that decision and rebuild the conditions that make investing in potential worthwhile. This is a management challenge, and it requires management solutions.

远程工作的隐性成本并非不可避免。这些成本之所以出现，是因为许多组织改变了员工的工作地点，却没有重新思考员工如何学习、发展以及建立有意义的联系。在招聘时选择有经验的候选人而非有潜力的人才，这种情况仍会继续发生。但是，当管理者理解了这一决策背后的因素，并重新构建值得投资于潜力员工的条件时，他们可能会做出不同且更优的选择。这是一项管理挑战，需要管理层面的解决方案。
