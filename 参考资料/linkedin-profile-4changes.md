# LinkedIn 改了 4 个地方，回复率明显提高了
# 4 LinkedIn Profile Changes That Noticeably Improved My Reply Rate

## 一句话总结 / TL;DR

> 很多人每天投几十份简历，却很少有人认真优化 LinkedIn。后来发现，**很多 HR 是先点开你主页，再决定要不要联系你**——所以主页就是你的第二份简历。

> Most people blast dozens of applications a day but rarely optimize their LinkedIn. I found many recruiters **open your profile first, then decide whether to reach out** — so your profile is effectively your second resume.

---

## 改动一 · 清晰、专业、有活力的头像 / 1. A clear, professional, energetic headshot

**中文**：作者靠加入学生组织免费拿到一张不错的职业照；双臂交叉、穿着西装、显得自信、有活力。

**English**: I got a decent professional photo for free by joining a student organization — arms crossed, suit on, looking confident and energetic.

---

## 改动二 · Headline 别只写“学生” / 2. Don't let your Headline just say "Student"

**中文**：每个研究生都是学生，写“学生”没有区分度。改成你提供的价值 / 研究方向。
**关键**：搜索栏里**前几个字比句尾更重要**——把关键词放前面。例如想做顾问或数据分析师，先写“顾问 / 数据分析师”，再列技能（如 Python），再放专业（如公共卫生）。

**English**: Every grad student is a student — "Student" says nothing. State the value you provide or your field instead.
**Key**: In search, the **first few words matter more than the end of the sentence** — put keywords first. E.g., if you want to be a consultant or data analyst, lead with "Consultant / Data Analyst", then skills (Python), then your major (Public Health).

---

## 改动三 · About 用 AI 辅助写，但要真实 / 3. Write the About section with AI help — but keep it real

**中文**：可以借 AI 写一段介绍（没人拦你，也不算假资料）。但必须说清三件事：**你是谁、你做什么、有哪些可证明的结果**。量化你的成就，作为简历之外的“点睛之笔”——大多数人简历只有一页，LinkedIn 正好作申请的补充。

**English**: You can use AI to draft the intro (no one stops you, and it's not fake). But state three things clearly: **who you are, what you do, and proof/results**. Quantify achievements as the finishing touch beyond your one-page resume — LinkedIn is the perfect supplement to applications.

---

## 改动四 · Featured 栏目展示项目 / 课程 / 证书 / 4. Showcase projects / courses / certs in Featured

**中文**：在 Featured 里加项目仪表盘、实习下的课程、志愿工作、证书（如 Google 数据分析、市场营销网课）。
**尤其适合转行或空档期**：证明“你在受训、让时间有价值”，而不是躺着无所事事；让招聘者**一眼看到你的技能**。

**English**: In Featured, add project dashboards, courses under experience, volunteer work, and certificates (Google Data Analytics, marketing courses).
**Especially useful during a career switch or gap**: it proves you're training and making your time valuable instead of lying idle — and puts your skills right in front of recruiters.

## 适用人群 / Who it's for

- 留学生 / 北美求职 / 外企求职，想提升 LinkedIn 被 HR 主动联系概率的人
- 简历只有一页、需要 LinkedIn 作补充展示的人
- 转行 / 空档期，想让“空白期”显得有价值的人

- International / remote job seekers who want recruiters to reach out proactively
- Anyone whose one-page resume needs a richer supplement
- Career switchers / gap-period applicants who want to make downtime look valuable
